## Data Watch for Android

A continuous alarm when your mobile data download + upload speed exceeds 1 MB per second.

### [Download & install Data Watch — 66 KB APK](https://github.com/AYASHI-Company/Projects/releases/download/datawatch-v1.0.0/DataWatch.apk)

Open the link on your **Android phone (Android 8+)**. No GitHub sign-in or ZIP extraction is needed.

1. Download **DataWatch.apk**, then open it.
2. If Android asks, allow installation from your browser or Files app and tap **Install**.
3. Open **Data Watch**, allow notifications, and adjust your phone’s **alarm volume**.
4. Tap **Test continuous alarm**, then **Stop alarm & monitoring** to silence the test.
5. Leave the threshold at **1 MB/s** and tap **Start monitoring**.

The alarm keeps playing until you stop it, even if the data speed drops. Mobile data is selected by default; Wi-Fi monitoring is optional.

[Release page and installation details](https://github.com/AYASHI-Company/Projects/releases/tag/datawatch-v1.0.0)

### Background monitoring

Allow unrestricted battery use for Data Watch in Android app settings. Monitoring uses battery and must be started again after a reboot or force stop. Phone sound settings and battery management can affect operation.

No ads and no internet permission. Built and signature-verified on GitHub, with eight rate-calculation checks passed. Real-phone behavior has not yet been tested.
